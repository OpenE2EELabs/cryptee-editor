cryptee-editor

cryptee-editor is a standalone open-source browser editor for end-to-end encrypted DOCX, XLSX, and PPTX documents.

It is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Source code: https://github.com/OpenE2EELabs/cryptee-editor.

Built on

  • CryptPad onlyoffice-x2t-wasm
  • CryptPad onlyoffice-editor
  • CryptPad ChainPad
  • CryptPad
  • ONLYOFFICE

Privacy properties and vulnerability reporting are documented in docs/SECURITY.md.
